## Narrative copy

### Opening

- Eyebrow: `MODEL UNITED NATIONS / CONCEPT 01`
- Headline: `The world doesn’t arrive at consensus. You draft it.`
- Body: `Bring a position. Test it in public. Leave with language that can hold more than one truth.`
- Status rail: `Draft 00 / Open for amendment / Briefing available`

### Mandate

- Heading: `Diplomacy is a writing discipline.`
- Lead: `A speech can move a room. A clause can move the record.`
- Sequence:
  1. `Listen for the interest beneath the position.`
  2. `Contest the language, not the person.`
  3. `Amend until more delegates can live inside the sentence.`
  4. `Vote with a record of what changed.`

### Committees — all visibly illustrative

- **DISEC / Disarmament:** `Regulate autonomous weapons without freezing legitimate research.`
  - Pressure: `security vs. enforceability`
- **UNEP / Environment:** `Allocate climate-loss financing when responsibility and vulnerability diverge.`
  - Pressure: `urgency vs. burden-sharing`
- **CRISIS / Cabinet:** `Stabilize a rapidly changing situation while information remains incomplete.`
  - Pressure: `speed vs. legitimacy`

### Procedure

1. Research — `Know the mandate, country position, red lines, and negotiable ground.`
2. Caucus — `Listen for coalitions that do not yet have common language.`
3. Draft — `Turn shared interests into clauses with an actor, action, and standard.`
4. Amend — `Change the text without erasing the coalition that made it possible.`
5. Vote — `Make the final disagreement legible.`

### Delegate desk

- Heading: `Arrive with more than a speech.`
- Checklist items:
  - `I can explain my assigned position in two sentences.`
  - `I have identified one red line and two negotiable points.`
  - `I have written one operative clause with an actor and action.`
  - `I can name three delegations whose interests may overlap mine.`
  - `I know how I will ask for clarification, yield, and propose an amendment.`

### Illustrative day

### Closing

- Heading: `Do not wait for consensus. Write the first sentence it can enter.`
- Body: `This concept is ready to be fitted to a real conference once the organizer, dates, venue, committees, fees, safeguarding, accessibility, and registration system are verified.`
- Action: `Open the build briefing`
